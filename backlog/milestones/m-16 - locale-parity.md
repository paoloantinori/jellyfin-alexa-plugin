## Description

Findings from the 2026-08-23 external-reviewer assessment of the interaction models. The 16 non-it-IT locales have structural sample starvation (FindSong 5 vs 12, PlaySong 10 vs 38), dialog registration asymmetry, and almost no NLU/E2E test coverage. Goal: bring secondary locales to a defensible quality floor, it-IT remains the reference.
