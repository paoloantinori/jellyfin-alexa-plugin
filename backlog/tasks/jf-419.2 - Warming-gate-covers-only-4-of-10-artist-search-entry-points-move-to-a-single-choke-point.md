## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Code-review finding (2026-08-31, high effort) on the JF-419 implementation. BaseHandler.cs:1492 copied per handler.

DEFECT: the warming gate is copy-pasted into only 4 handlers while 6 other artist-search entry points still hit the slow cold-index DB path: AddToQueueIntent, MediaInfoIntent, PlayMoodMusic (via BaseHandler.TryEntityFallbackAsync), PlayNextIntent, QueryArtistLibraryIntent, SearchMediaIntent. During the cold-start window the user gets the friendly warming message for "suona i pink floyd" but an 8s+ stall and on-device INVALID_RESPONSE for "quali album ha pink floyd", "aggiungi pink floyd alla coda", or a PlayMoodMusic miss routing through TryEntityFallbackAsync. Same live-incident class JF-419 was built from, still reachable.

FIX SHAPE: move the gate to one choke point instead of per-handler copies. Natural candidates: ArtistSearch.SearchAsync (all artist searches funnel there) or the request pipeline. Watch the two documented copies of the search chain (ArtistSearch.SearchAsync + inline PlayArtistSongs Thorough/Fast mode, JF-382): the choke point must cover BOTH, including the Fast-mode DB path.
