## Description

Filed same-turn by the JF-844 es-episode-surgery worker from the /simplify altitude review (2026-10-09). The JF-844 densification added 7 identical season-ed PlayEpisodeIntent rows to templates/es-ES.yaml, es-MX.yaml, es-US.yaml by hand: the es locales are verbatim-transcription templates with no shared-include mechanism (the JF-316 golden-master decision), so the mirroring is maintained by hand and nothing mechanical guards it. The validator's Phase 5 guards template-to-JSON sync only; a future single-locale edit to one es season-ed family (exactly the class commit 22fed1ab performed deliberately for the connector rows) would drift silently, and today the only record of the intended deltas is hand-maintained prose in the template headers' divergence lists. Value: turn the es-trio PlayEpisode mirroring invariant into a checked lint instead of prose. Suggested shape: a warning-level lint in scripts/validate_interaction_models.py asserting es-MX and es-US PlayEpisodeIntent samples are a subset of es-ES's, modulo the enumerated divergences (after JF-844: only the three 'que reproduzca' connector rows, es-ES-only pending its live probe; plus the pre-existing per-locale divergences that touch other intents: es-US's English FindSong sets, the SeriesName last-entry swap). Keep the whitelist as data in the validator, not prose. Out of scope: any general cross-locale mirroring framework; this is the one intent where a deliberate trio design exists.
