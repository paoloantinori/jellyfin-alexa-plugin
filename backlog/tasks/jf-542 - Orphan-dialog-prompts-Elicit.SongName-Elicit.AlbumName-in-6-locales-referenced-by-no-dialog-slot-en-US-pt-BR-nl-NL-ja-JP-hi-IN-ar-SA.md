## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Six committed interaction models define prompts Elicit.SongName and Elicit.AlbumName that no dialog slot references (verified 2026-09-11 by the JF-316 milestone-4 review across all 17 models): en-US, pt-BR, nl-NL, ja-JP, hi-IN, ar-SA. The other 11 locales carry no prompts section at all. The prompts are inert (Alexa ignores prompt definitions not referenced by any dialog slot; the code-driven ElicitSlot flow depends on the intent's dialog.intents registration, which exists), so they are dead weight plus a cross-locale shape drift, invisible to validators and to SMAPI. Found during the JF-316 milestone-4 golden-master review; deliberately NOT cleaned there because the milestone contract is byte-identical transcription of the hand-grown models. Cleanup means deciding per family whether to delete the two orphan definitions (and the surrounding prompts section if it becomes empty) or to wire them to real dialog slots, then regenerating. Affected templated locales (edit template + regen): en-US, pt-BR. Hand-maintained: nl-NL, ja-JP, hi-IN, ar-SA (edit JSON directly). The pt-BR template header documents the orphan state with a do-not-clean-up note; that note must be updated in the same change if the orphans are removed.
